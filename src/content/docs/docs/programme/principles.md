These are commitments, not shipped features.

## Local-first, everything in your project

Config, history and decisions live in git, on your machine. troupe doesn't need a hosted service to work.

## Built for teams of agents and people

People hold roles the same way agents do, so each question goes to the person who owns it, not to whoever
happens to be watching.

## Escalation that does its homework

Before an agent asks anyone, it runs the research steps your project defines: check the issues, read the docs,
look up the relevant standard. Then it brings a decision brief, not a bare question.

## Lineage for every decision

Every decision records who made it, human or agent, what it was based on, who approved it, and where its
results went.

## Bring your own agent

Agents are pluggable: use ours, or bring your own. The process doesn't depend on one agent, so you can swap
Claude Code for another without rewriting it.
